# Mira 0.5.4

Mira 0.5.4 upgrades high-award writing with innovation and contribution
concentration.

## Added Capability

**Innovation/contribution ledger**

Strong contest papers should make their contribution easy to find. Mira should
extract real contributions before final writing and connect each one to
evidence.

Preferred artifacts:

- `planning/innovation_ledger.md`
- `results/tables/contribution_ledger.csv`

Preferred paper sections:

- `本文创新与贡献`
- `模型创新点`
- `主要贡献`
- `方法优势与证据`

Each contribution should state:

1. what is new or unusually strong;
2. what ordinary-model weakness it fixes;
3. what evidence supports it;
4. what benefit it creates;
5. where the claim is valid.

## New Gate Behavior

The award gate checks whether papers with innovation claims have a named
contribution section or contribution artifact. Scattered praise without
evidence-backed contribution items is treated as a high-award warning.
